The setup

A services company that had built plenty for its clients set out to build something of its own: AIGINT, an AI-governance platform for enterprise teams. I led the UX strategy and interaction design — competitive research, a modular service architecture to support customized deployments, the full working UI prototype, and the design principles for handing off from consultant to app.

From documentation to a working product

The starting point was a large, unstructured body of internal documentation. In a month it was a clickable prototype; a couple of months later, a working one — users could step through governance topics and export a generated PDF report. I vibe-coded the front end with Claude in VS Code, which let a very small team move at a pace a small team usually can’t.

What it proved

The prototype was compelling enough to secure signed client commitments — about $1.1M — before launch. It was ultimately not carried to market. What it demonstrated stands on its own: how fast AI-assisted design can take an idea from raw documentation to a product customers will commit to.
