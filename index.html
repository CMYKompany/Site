LEDGER

Flagship · AI experience design

Designing trust into an AI assistant for 250,000 people.

Sole UI/UX owner for a DHS policy chatbot. Usability testing with policy knowledge-workers proved that source transparency and response precision were non-negotiable — findings that reshaped the interaction model, drove architecture changes to support multi-turn conversation, and produced a CRIT-based prompting guide that doubled as the UAT test suite. I built consensus across a skeptical coalition of policy, legal, and executive stakeholders — and shipped.

  • 250K users served
  • 1 sole UI/UX owner
  • 3 stakeholder camps aligned
