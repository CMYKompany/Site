How I lead

I care about you, and I want to hear your story. I ask a lot of questions, of a lot of people. I focus on people and process—then results. (…and of course, I make the results pretty.)

Today I mentor 20+ designers and researchers, and the practice I built now supports 24 programs and $70M+ in new design work at ECS — its CX/UX Center of Excellence, which I stood up from nothing.

  • I authored the career tracks and role definitions my designers are measured against — and coached six of them to promotion.
  • I hold interview veto across designer hiring: 21 hires in two years, about a third of the design staff.
  • At the U.S. Treasury I held delegated brand authority under Treasury Order 100-01, and wrote the department’s first Visual Identity Guidelines and Style Guide.
